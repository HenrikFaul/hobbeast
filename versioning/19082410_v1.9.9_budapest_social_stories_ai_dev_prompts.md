## Product and design decision

Reference products informed four principles only: bold editorial hierarchy, people before
places, playful identity labels and generous visual rhythm. Hobbeast does not reproduce their
copy, screenshots, layout or brand. The home message is now the original statement “A város
tele van közös történetekkel.” and the four scenes form one ownable Budapest narrative.

## Non-regression contracts

- Preserve every route, URL parameter, handler, auth rule, organizer/admin boundary, event
  lifecycle action, trust control, disclosure and privacy behavior.
- New visuals may add entry points, but may not create a parallel fake discovery or event flow.
- Poster delivery must remain functional when video codecs, autoplay, bandwidth or motion
  preferences block enhancement.
- No third-party imagery, competitor frames, logos, testimonials or translated slogans.
- Keep all images below 256 KB; require each video below 1.5 MB and both variants below 2.5 MB.
- Treat synthetic adults as editorial illustration, never as member testimony or documentary
  proof of a named Budapest venue.
