- **Scope:** deepen the existing Hobbeast visual system with real activity photography,

  replacing any working product capability.

## Business request

Continue the premium redesign in Hobbeast's own brand. Replace flat hobby tiles with faded,
category-relevant real photographs; use wide hero gutters for tasteful activity stories;
retain both day and night hero moods and vary them by context; remove fake pan/zoom motion in
favour of short real clips; and make connection, shared learning, growth, mutual support and
the joy of being together more visible across the experience. Use licensed free stock rather
than ImageGen for this round, then verify, version, commit, push and deploy without regression.

## Product and design decision

Competitor examples informed only broad principles: confident editorial hierarchy, people
before places, emotional clarity, generous rhythm and activity-led discovery. Hobbeast keeps
its own dark-green, acid-lime, coral and lavender identity, original Hungarian copy, complete
feature depth and existing route/state contracts. Peace and love appear only as small,
inclusive community motifs rather than borrowed counterculture branding.

## Implemented expansion

1. Add one locally hosted Pexels-derived WebP photograph to each of the 17 top-level hobby
   cards. Keep the full `HOBBY_CATALOG` domain model, ordering, IDs and 17/50/263 counts intact.
2. Preserve each Explore card as one native button with its existing accessible label and
   category → subcategory → activity → back state machine. Use a tone wash and glass reading
   panel so imagery supports rather than competes with the information.
3. Replace the static pan/zoom hero derivatives with two genuine, audio-free Pexels clips:
   one daytime walking scene and one night sparkler celebration. Normalize both to 1280×720
   web-optimized H.264 MP4, preserve their verified cadence honestly and keep them below
   1.5 MB each.
4. Select day/night presentation with season-aware daylight windows plus a small editorial
   variation, store the result per day/session, and load only the selected clip dynamically.
5. Preserve poster-first LCP, mobile static delivery, Save-Data/2G exclusion,
   `prefers-reduced-motion`, visibility/intersection pause, user pause state and keyboard
   controls. Failed or blocked motion must degrade to the existing still.
6. Use otherwise empty ultra-wide hero gutters only above 1820 px for climbing and shared
   gaming stories. Every card enters the existing `/events?q=...&mode=search` flow.
7. Add the “Nyerjük vissza egymást.” manifesto and strengthen existing home copy around
   shared learning, development, movement, making and helping. Keep “A hétvégéd nem egy feed.
   Menj, és éld meg.” as the principal behavioural statement.
8. Present WHO, Harvard and PLOS material with cautious wording: relationship quality and
   mutual support are associated with well-being; Hobbeast creates meeting opportunities but
   does not guarantee happiness, health, friendship or longevity. Label the referenced book
   as inspiration rather than evidence.
9. Record creator, source page, license URL, transform policy, metadata, byte size and SHA-256
   for the licensed stock. For the new day mobile poster, record the provable local
   parent/derivative lineage and disclose the legacy parent's unresolved upstream provenance.
   Serve committed local derivatives and never production hotlinks.
10. Add deterministic hero-selection unit tests, full photographic Explore state-machine E2E
    coverage and a configurable Playwright base URL for isolated local regression runs.

## Non-regression contracts

- Preserve all routes, query parameters, handlers, authorization rules, organizer/admin
  controls, event lifecycle actions, trust controls, disclosures and privacy boundaries.
- Do not move presentation-only imagery into the shared hobby domain model or alter category
  IDs, order, counts or search behaviour.
- Do not request hero video on mobile, reduced-motion, Save-Data or constrained-network
  clients. Do not mount two variants or create a carousel.
- Keep every new image below 256 KB; each video below 1.5 MB; exactly two hero clips together
  below 2.5 MB; landing main JavaScript below 50 KiB gzip.
- Never imply that stock subjects are Hobbeast members or endorse the product. Preserve the
  Pexels source/license ledger and avoid competitor media, copy, logos or composition clones.
- A skipped authenticated fixture is `NOT_RUN`, never production proof.
